Research

My research interests are in embodied intelligence, with the goal of advancing robots to understand and interact with the physical world. My work in multimodal perception, state estimation, efficient inference, VLMs, robot autonomy and computational neuroscience has shaped my interests in:

  • Multimodal World Modeling: How can world models learn grounded physical dynamics from exteroceptive observations (vision, audio, touch), proprioception, and language?
  • Hierarchical Planning and Control: How can these grounded models couple hierarchical reasoning and planning with closed-loop control for reliable action, failure anticipation, and recovery on long-horizon tasks?
